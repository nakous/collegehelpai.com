### 📋 Before Contacting Support

**To help us help you faster, please:**

#### ✅ Check These Resources First
1. **[FAQ Section](../faq/)** - Common questions and answers
2. **[Troubleshooting Guide](../troubleshooting/)** - Fix common issues yourself
3. **[Installation Verification](../installation/verification.md)** - Ensure proper setup

#### 📝 Information to Include
When contacting support, please provide:

**Basic Information:**
- Your browser type and version
- Extension version (found in popup footer)
- Operating system (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- Description of the issue

**For Technical Issues:**
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- Error messages (exact text or screenshots)
- What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened

**For Billing Issues:**
- Your account email address
- Transaction ID or receipt number
- Billing date and amount

- **Be specific** in your description
