## Welcome to AI College Help!

AI College Help is a revolutionary browser extension designed to help students excel in their studies. Using advanced artificial intelligence, our extension analyzes your questions, solves mathematical problems, and provides detailed explanations to help you better understand your coursework.

### 🌟 Key Features

- **🔍 Smart Text Analysis** - Select any text and get instant explanations
- **📸 Screenshot Solver** - Take photos of your problems and receive step-by-step solutions
- **🌍 Multilingual Support** - Available in 6 languages (EN, FR, ES, IT, AR, RU)
- **⚡ Keyboard Shortcuts** - Quick access with Ctrl+Shift+A and Ctrl+Shift+S
- **🎯 Camouflage Mode** - Discreet usage during your study sessions
- **🔒 Secure and Private** - Your data is protected and encrypted
