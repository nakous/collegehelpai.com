### ⭐ Top 10 Most Asked Questions

#### 1. **How do I install AI College Help?**
📖 **Answer:** Visit our [installation guide](../installation/) for step-by-step instructions for Chrome and Edge.

#### 2. **Is AI College Help free?**
💰 **Answer:** We offer a free plan with 10 questions per day. See our [pricing page](https://collegehelpai.com/#pricing) for premium plans.

#### 3. **What languages are supported?**
🌐 **Answer:** AI College Help supports 6 languages: English, French, Spanish, Italian, Arabic, and Russian for both interface and responses.

#### 4. **How do I take a screenshot?**
📸 **Answer:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+S` or click the screenshot button in the popup. See our [screenshot guide](../usage/screenshot.md).

#### 5. **Why isn't the extension working?**
🔧 **Answer:** Check that permissions are granted, your browser is updated, and you're connected to the internet. See [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/).

#### 6. **Can I use it for exams?**
📚 **Answer:** Always check your institution's policies. AI College Help is designed for learning and study assistance.

#### 7. **How do I change the response language?**
⚙️ **Answer:** Go to Settings > Languages and select your preferred response language.

#### 8. **What is camouflage mode?**
🎭 **Answer:** A discreet mode that makes the extension less visible. Learn more in our [camouflage guide](../configuration/camouflage-mode.md).

#### 9. **How do I contact support?**
💬 **Answer:** Visit our [contact page](https://collegehelpai.com/contact-us/) or email us directly.

#### 10. **Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
🔄 **Answer:** Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.

### 🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting

#### Extension Issues
- **Not working?** → Check [installation verification](../installation/verification.md)
- **No response?** → Verify internet connection and account status
- **Wrong language?** → Adjust language settings

#### Account Issues
- **Can't sign in?** → Reset password or check email/username
- **Billing questions?** → Visit account settings or contact support
- **Usage limits?** → Check your current plan and usage
