# 💰 Account and Billing FAQ

## Account Management and Subscription Questions

### 🔐 Account Creation and Management

#### **Q: How do I create an account?**
**A:** 
1. Click the AI College Help extension icon
2. Click "Register" or "Sign Up"
3. You'll be redirected to [collegehelpai.com](https://collegehelpai.com)
4. Fill in your email, password, and basic information
5. Verify your email address
6. Return to the extension and sign in

#### **Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
**A:** 
1. Go to the sign-in page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for reset instructions
5. Follow the link to create a new password

#### **Q: Can I change my email address?**
**A:** Yes, you can update your email in your account settings on the website. You'll need to verify the new email address.

#### **Q: How do I delete my account?**
**A:** Contact our support team at [collegehelpai.com/contact-us/](https://collegehelpai.com/contact-us/) to request account deletion. We'll process your request within 48 hours.

### 💳 Billing and Subscriptions

#### **Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
**A:** We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay (on supported devices)
- Google Pay (on supported devices)

#### **Q: How much does AI College Help cost?**
**A:** Our current pricing is:
- **Free Plan:** $0/month - 10 questions per day
- **Student Plan:** $9.99/month - 100 questions per day + advanced features
- **Premium Plan:** $19.99/month - Unlimited questions + all features

📋 **[View detailed pricing](https://collegehelpai.com/#pricing)**

#### **Q: Is there a student discount?**
**A:** Yes! Our Student Plan is already discounted for students. Contact support with your .edu email for additional verification and potential discounts.

#### **Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
**A:** Absolutely! You can cancel your subscription at any time:
1. Log into your account on collegehelpai.com
2. Go to Subscription Settings
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Confirm cancellation

Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

#### **Q: What happens when I cancel?**
**A:** 
- Your subscription continues until the current period ends
- You keep premium features until expiration
- After expiration, you revert to the free plan
- Your data and settings are preserved

### 📊 Usage and Limits

#### **Q: How do I check my usage?**
**A:** Your current usage is displayed:
- In the extension popup (questions remaining)
- On your account dashboard on the website
- Via email notifications when approaching limits

#### **Q: What counts as a 'question'?**
**A:** One question equals:
- One text analysis request
- One screenshot analysis request
- One retry of the same content doesn't count as additional

#### **Q: What happens if I exceed my limit?**
**A:** 
- Free users: Wait until next day or upgrade to paid plan
- Paid users: Contact support if you consistently exceed limits

#### **Q: Do unused questions roll over?**
**A:** No, daily question limits reset each day. Monthly limits reset on your billing cycle date.

### 🔄 Billing Cycles and Refunds

#### **Q: When am I billed?**
**A:** 
- Monthly plans: Billed on the same date each month
- Annual plans: Billed once per year with savings
- Billing occurs automatically on your renewal date

#### **Q: Can I get a refund?**
**A:** We offer:
- 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions
- Prorated refunds for annual plans (case-by-case basis)
- No refunds for partial month usage

#### **Q: Why was I charged twice?**
**A:** This might happen if:
- You upgraded mid-cycle (you'll see prorated charges)
- Your payment failed and was retried
- You have multiple active subscriptions

Contact support immediately if you see duplicate charges.

#### **Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade?**
**A:** 
1. Log into your account
2. Go to Subscription Settings
3. Choose new plan
4. Changes take effect immediately (upgrades) or next billing cycle (downgrades)
