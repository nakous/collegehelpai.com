# 📸 Screenshot Capture and Image Analysis

## Solve Your Visual Problems with One Click

AI College Help's screenshot feature allows you to analyze visual content: handwritten equations, graphs, diagrams, and much more.

### 🎯 What is AI Screenshot Analysis?

This feature uses optical character recognition (OCR) and artificial intelligence to:
- **Read** handwritten or printed equations
- **Analyze** graphs and diagrams
- **Solve** visual mathematical problems
- **Explain** scientific schemas
- **Interpret** tables and data

### ⚡ Capture Optimization

#### Tips for Better Results

**Image Quality:**
- ✅ **Select precisely** the area of interest
- ✅ **Avoid areas too small** (minimum 100x100 pixels)
- ✅ **Include necessary context** but not too much
- ✅ **Ensure** content sharpness

**Optimal Conditions:**
- 🔆 **Good screen brightness**
- 📱 **Appropriate zoom** (not too small, not too big)
- 🎯 **Sufficient contrast** between text and background
- ✨ **Non-blurred** or pixelated image

#### Recommended Capture Sizes

| Content Type | Minimum Size | Optimal Size |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Simple equation** | 200x50 px | 400x100 px |
| **Graph** | 300x200 px | 600x400 px |
| **Table** | 250x150 px | 500x300 px |
| **Diagram** | 300x300 px | 600x600 px |

### 🔧 Troubleshooting

#### Common Issues

**"No content detected":**
- Verify the area contains analyzable text/content
- Increase selection size
- Improve screen contrast

**"Image too blurry":**
- Increase page zoom
- Ensure original is sharp
- Avoid captures of already compressed images

**"Incomplete analysis":**
- Include more context in capture
- Separate complex elements into multiple captures
- Verify all elements are visible
